That's the question I keep coming back to: how does rapid technological change reshape the way people perceive and judge products? I've spent three years in industry watching strategy lag behind consumer reality, and that gap is what pulled me toward research. I've always been drawn to psychology broadly, not just in marketing contexts, and consumer behavior is where that interest found traction.
